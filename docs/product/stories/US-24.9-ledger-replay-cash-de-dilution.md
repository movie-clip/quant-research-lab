# US-24.9: Cash de-dilution on the imported ledger-replay return series

**Epic:** Epic 24 — Codebase Improvement
**PRD:** [`epic-24-codebase-improvement.md`](../prd/epic-24-codebase-improvement.md)
**Status:** Done
**Last updated:** 2026-08-09

## Story

As a **portfolio researcher**, I want the **risk statistics computed from my
imported broker replay** (beta, correlation, annualised volatility, the
volatility regime, and the statistical factor model) to measure **the
performance of my holdings**, not a series diluted by the idle cash sitting in
the account, so that the imported portfolio's risk numbers are comparable with
the synthetic-history surfaces that already exclude cash — and with the
benchmark, which has no cash at all.

## Context

Tech-debt register row **US-24.9 — cash de-dilution on the imported
ledger-replay return series**, logged by US-30.5c (Epic 30, PRD F-10),
renumbered from 24.8 by US-24.6, marked **blocked by Epic 31** on 2026-07-18 and
**unblocked 2026-08-04** when Epic 31 closed (F-1..F-5 all resolved — the
replayed series is now trustworthy enough to refine).

US-30.5c introduced the provenance-selected `ReturnBasis`
(`app/analytics/risk.py:58`) and moved every **synthetic** surface onto the
cash-excluded market-value chain `r_t = MV_t / MV_{t−1} − 1`. The **imported
ledger-replay** path (`provenance.historical_basis ==
"imported_portfolio_history"`) deliberately stayed on the cash-inclusive TWR
`r_t = (PV_t − external_cash_flow_t) / PV_{t−1} − 1`, because a plain
market-value chain there reads a BUY as a gain — the audit F-1 fabrication class
(reproduced on IB2026: **+37.23% on the 2026-06-19 trade day**). That was the
right call, but it leaves a real distortion: on the ledger path `PV = MV + cash`,
so every equity return is scaled down by the cash weight (~3% of NAV on IB2026),
understating beta by roughly the same fraction (the US-30.4 F-10 measurement:
scale 0.9805 → beta understated ~1.95%).

The fix both prior stories named but could not build is a **per-day trade-flow
field** on `DailyPortfolioState`, enabling a **trade-neutral market-value
chain**:

```text
r_t = (MV_t − trade_flow_t) / MV_{t−1} − 1
```

where `trade_flow_t` is the net market value **injected into the holdings by
trades settled on day t** (BUY positive, SELL negative) — i.e. the negation of
those entries' base-currency `cash_effect`. Subtracting it removes exactly the
leg the market-value chain would otherwise read as performance, while the
denominator excludes cash. `PortfolioStateEngine.build_daily_states` already
computes the converted per-entry amount it needs
(`app/engine/portfolio_state.py:273-284`) and already separates BUY/SELL from
`DEPOSIT`/`WITHDRAWAL` — it simply discards the trade total instead of recording
it.

**Scope boundary that must hold.** This story changes the **risk-statistic**
family only (the surfaces `diagnostics_engine.py:398-418` selects a basis for).
The **investor-performance** family stays on the cash-inclusive TWR by design:
`build_true_performance_series` (the Dashboard performance chart),
`build_performance_summary` (TWR / money-weighted return / net contributions),
the monthly-return chain, and `_build_dashboard_max_drawdown`
(`dashboard_history_engine.py:881-898`, deliberately put on TWR by US-27.2 / F2
so a deposit cannot mask a drawdown). Those answer "what did the investor earn
on the money in the account", where cash is part of the answer.

Implementer must read: `docs/finance/financial-methodology.md` §Rolling Pearson
Correlation (its closing paragraph names this exact deferral), §Indexed Return
Series, §Portfolio Return Methodology; `docs/contracts/dashboard-fields.md`
(the `DailyPortfolioState` rows added by US-31.3);
`app/engine/portfolio_state.py:255-325`; `app/analytics/risk.py:44-58` and
`_portfolio_time_weighted_return_series` (`:1566-1612`);
`app/analytics/attribution.py:59-98`; `app/services/diagnostics_engine.py:392-418`;
the tech-debt register row US-24.9.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — Every replayed daily state carries a **`trade_flow`** figure: the
  net base-currency market value moved into holdings by BUY/SELL entries settled
  that day (BUY positive, SELL negative), `0.0` on a day with no trades. It is
  recorded for **all** `PortfolioStateEngine` callers (dashboard history,
  diagnostics, drift, proof), not only the diagnostics path, and it is never
  `null` in place of `0.0`.
- [x] AC2 — `trade_flow` is **FX-converted per entry** using the same
  `to_base_currency` conversion the cash ledger already applies; the raw
  currency-mixed `cash_effect` sum is **not** an acceptable basis (the US-31.3
  measurement trap — on IB2026 the raw sum is −$271.23 against −$2,459.29
  converted). A dedicated test rejects the raw basis.
- [x] AC3 — On the **imported ledger-replay** path, the portfolio return series
  feeding beta, correlation, annualised volatility, relative risk (tracking
  error / active return / information ratio), the volatility regime and the
  statistical factor model is the **trade-neutral market-value chain**
  `r_t = (MV_t − trade_flow_t)/MV_{t−1} − 1` — cash excluded from both numerator
  and denominator.
- [x] AC4 — **No fabrication on trade days** (the guardrail this whole basis
  exists for): on IB2026's largest trade day the published return under the new
  basis is a plausible market move, **not** the double-digit figure a naive
  market-value chain produces there; the story pins that day's value explicitly.
  *(Date corrected during implementation: this AC was written citing US-30.5c's
  **2026-06-19 / +37.23%**. That date is **not a valuation date** on the current
  statement window — it is a US market holiday, so the frozen SPY series has no
  row for it and the replay never values it; the cited figure predates Epic 31's
  changes to the window. The live equivalent is **2026-04-14**: naive
  **+17.19%** vs trade-neutral **+2.64%**, pinned in
  `test_ledger_replay_audit.py`.)*
- [x] AC5 — **Synthetic surfaces are unchanged.** A series built from
  `market_data_history` (and the always-synthetic attribution / stress /
  multi-benchmark surfaces) still uses the plain `market_value` chain; because
  such a series has no trades, `trade_flow ≡ 0.0` and the two chains are
  numerically identical — asserted, not assumed.
- [x] AC6 — **The investor-performance family is untouched.** The Dashboard
  performance series, TWR, money-weighted return, net contributions, monthly
  returns and the dashboard max-drawdown still use the cash-inclusive TWR
  `(PV_t − external_cash_flow_t)/PV_{t−1} − 1`; their golden values are
  **unchanged**.
- [x] AC7 — **Withholding still wins.** A day whose state carries a material
  `reconciliation_adjustment` (`return_is_publishable == False`, US-31.3 / F-3)
  publishes **no** return under the new basis either — the trade-neutral chain
  does not become a way to smuggle an accounting correction back into a return
  series.
- [x] AC8 — **Fail-closed, not fabricated:** where `MV_{t−1}` is zero the day
  yields no return (skipped, as today) rather than a divide-by-zero or a
  substituted value; a day whose trade flow cannot be converted to the base
  currency is treated as unconvertible and disclosed through the existing
  FX-fallback disclosure rather than silently converted 1:1.
- [x] AC9 — The **measured effect is recorded** in the slice log: the IB2026
  before/after annualised volatility, beta and correlation under the old vs new
  basis, so the cash de-dilution is a demonstrated number, not a claim. If any
  figure moves by more than the cash weight can explain, that is a finding to
  investigate before shipping, not a result to accept.
  *(The tripwire **fired twice** and both were investigated before shipping —
  see Notes → "What the AC9 measurement found". One was a defect in this

- [x] AC11 — Methodology §Rolling Pearson Correlation's closing "deliberately

## Out of scope

- **Changing any investor-performance number.** TWR, money-weighted return, net
  contributions, monthly returns, the Dashboard performance chart and the
  dashboard max-drawdown keep the cash-inclusive basis (AC6). A cash-excluded
  investor return would be a different, wrong claim about what the investor
  earned.
- **Modelling trade costs separately.** A BUY's `cash_effect` includes
  commissions and fees, so `trade_flow` neutralises cost-inclusive amounts; the
  fee portion remains a (correct, tiny) drag in the return series. Splitting fees
  out needs a per-entry fee field the importers do not model — not this story.
- **Intraday flow timing.** Trades are neutralised at end-of-day, matching the
  existing `external_cash_flow_applied_before_end_of_day_measurement`
  convention. Modified-Dietz style intra-day weighting is a separate methodology
  change.
- **US-24.5 / US-24.7** — separate tech-debt rows.
- Any new card, chart or product surface (Epic 24 non-goal).

## Notes / decisions

- **Why a third basis rather than switching the ledger path to `market_value`:**
  US-30.5c already measured that swap — it fabricates +37.23% on the IB2026
  2026-06-19 trade day (the F-1 class). The trade-neutral chain is the *only*
  formulation that removes cash dilution without reintroducing that fabrication,
  which is precisely why it needs the new state field.
- **Sign convention (get this right once):** BUY has a negative `cash_effect`
  (cash leaves) and increases market value, so `trade_flow = −Σ cash_effect`
  over that day's BUY/SELL entries — positive for a net buy. The neutralisation
  then **subtracts**: `(MV_t − trade_flow_t)/MV_{t−1} − 1`. A sign error here
  doubles the error instead of cancelling it, so test (1) and (2) in
  `test_portfolio_state.py` assert the sign directly.
- **Known small basis mismatch, to be measured not ignored:** market values are
  converted at the fund currency via the US-31.5 `valuation_currency` rule while
  trade `cash_effect` is converted from the entry's cash currency at the
  statement-implied rate. For same-currency trades these agree; for a
  cross-currency line a small residual remains. AC9's "more than the cash weight
  explains" check is the tripwire for it.
- **Academic precedent** (for the methodology section — the formula is a
  standard flow-adjusted period return, not a new invention):
  - Bacon, C.R. (2008). *Practical Portfolio Performance Measurement and
    Attribution*, 2nd ed., Ch. 2 (Wiley) — time-weighted return with
    end-of-period external flow adjustment; the same construction applied to a
    market-value (rather than total-value) denominator.
  - CFA Institute (2020). *Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS) for
    Firms*, §2.A.2 — returns must be adjusted for external cash flows; a
    security purchase within the portfolio is an internal transfer, not
    performance.
  - Elton, Gruber, Brown & Goetzmann (2014). *Modern Portfolio Theory and
    Investment Analysis*, 9th ed., Ch. 4 — beta/correlation are estimated on the
    return series of the risky assets; a cash sleeve scales the series and
    biases beta toward zero, which is the distortion this story removes.
### What the AC9 measurement found (recorded during implementation)

The tripwire fired twice. Neither was absorbed.

**(1) A fabrication in this story's own first implementation — fixed.**
Counting *every* BUY/SELL in `trade_flow` neutralises legs that never entered
market value. IUFS, IUHC and BTEC are `unpriced_replay_symbols` on IB2026: they
contribute **$0** to `total_market_value` all window. Selling IUFS + IUHC for
**$5,341.92** on 2026-04-27 therefore moved no market value at all, but the
first implementation still "neutralised" it — fabricating **+9.43%** on a day
the priced book moved **+0.02%**. Fixed by gating `trade_flow` on a new
side-effect-free `is_valued(symbol, day)` predicate in `PortfolioStateEngine`;
pinned in both `test_portfolio_state.py` (synthetic GHOST symbol) and
`test_ledger_replay_audit.py` (the real 2026-04-27 / 2026-04-08 pair). This is
why the engine gained one test beyond the plan's five.

**(2) A pre-existing distortion of the cash-inclusive TWR — new finding, not
fixed here.** The same three unpriced symbols corrupt the **investor-performance**
TWR that this story deliberately leaves alone (AC6). Because their market value
is $0 but their trades move real cash, `PV` jumps when they are sold and drops
when they are bought:

| Date | Cash moved by unpriced-symbol trades | TWR return published |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-04-08 | −$5,092.82 (buying BTEC/IUFS/IUHC) | **−7.90%** |
| 2026-04-27 | +$5,341.92 (selling IUFS/IUHC) | **+9.61%** |

Both are fabrications: nothing was earned or lost. The Dashboard performance
chart, TWR, monthly returns and max drawdown all still carry them. The
trade-neutral basis is immune (it works purely in priced-market-value space),
which is why the headline volatility fell so far — see below. Logged in
`docs/tech-debt-register.md` as **US-24.10**; fixing it means either pricing
those symbols or withholding the affected days, and it is an
investor-performance change, out of this story's scope.

**The de-dilution itself, measured cleanly.** Comparing like-for-like with those
two contaminated days excluded, against a **median cash weight of 5.60%**
(not the ~3% the story assumed — that was the *terminal* weight; the window
ranges −1.91% to 17.53%):

| Statistic | TWR (old) | Trade-neutral (new) | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annualised volatility | 14.54% | 15.47% | ×1.064 |
| Cash-weight prediction | — | — | ×1.059 |

Explained. The **headline** figures move much further — volatility
23.32% → 15.86%, beta 0.552 → 0.860, correlation 0.343 → 0.787, r² 0.118 →
0.619, max daily move 9.61% → 2.95% — because finding (2)'s two fabricated
days dominated the old series. Those numbers are an improvement in accuracy,
not a change in the portfolio's risk.

**And the guard held:** the naive market-value chain, had the ledger path simply
adopted it, would report volatility **41.43%** and a **+17.19%** trade day —
confirming US-30.5c's decision not to make that swap.

- **Impact is a refinement, not a bug fix.** Cash is ~3% of NAV on IB2026 and
  TWR handles it correctly for performance; this story makes the *risk*
  statistics consistent with the synthetic surfaces and with the benchmark.
  Expect small, explainable moves — an outsized move means something else is
  wrong.
