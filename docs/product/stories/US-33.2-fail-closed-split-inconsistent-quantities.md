# US-33.2: Fail closed on split-inconsistent reconstructed quantities

**Epic:** Epic 33 — Corporate Actions & Replay Quantity Integrity
**PRD:** [`epic-33-corporate-actions-replay-integrity.md`](../prd/epic-33-corporate-actions-replay-integrity.md)
**Status:** Done (2026-08-13)
**Last updated:** 2026-08-12

## Story

As a **portfolio researcher**, I want the replay to withhold and name any
holding whose reconstructed quantity is denominated in inconsistent share
units, so that a corporate action inside my statement produces a disclosed gap
instead of a phantom position valued at a stale pre-split price.

## Context

PRD findings **F-1** (the opening-position roll-back sums pre- and post-split
quantities) and **F-2** (US-24.10's trade-price anchor then values those
phantom units at the stale pre-split price). On the committed IB2026 statement
this is LQQ: the roll-back produces a **199-unit** opening position that was
never held, and the anchor carries **€1,457.78** across a ~200:1 split, so the
replayed market value peaks at **$518,078.75** (2026-07-06) against a portfolio
whose statement `stock_total` is **$64,922.99**.

The detection signal is already in the ledger and is not marginal. Measured
across the 61 symbols in the committed statement that carry priced trades (of
68 replayed), the within-symbol trade-price ratio (max/min, same currency) is:

| Symbol | Price ratio | Reconstructed opening |
|---|---|---|
| **LQQ** | **218.10** (€9.069 … €1,977.94) | **199** |
| NFLX | 1.40 | 8 |
| ISLN | 1.36 | 0 |
| DEFS | 1.28 | 0 |
| … | ≤ 1.28 | — |

LQQ is the only symbol above 2.0, and the gap between it and the highest
legitimate ratio is **156×**. That margin is what makes a threshold test
defensible here.

The behaviour this story adds is the same fail-closed shape US-31.3 gave the
cash anchor: measure, classify, withhold above tolerance, and disclose the
reason — never publish a confident number derived from inputs the engine knows
are inconsistent.

Implementer must read:
- `docs/product/prd/epic-33-corporate-actions-replay-integrity.md` — F-1/F-2
  and the non-goals (no split-ratio inference, no corporate-action model).
- `app/engine/portfolio_state.py` — the roll-back

- `docs/finance/financial-methodology.md` §Synthetic History Coverage Rule —

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — The replay measures, per symbol, the ratio between the highest and
  lowest **broker execution price in a single currency** across that symbol's
  own ledger, and treats a ratio at or above a documented threshold as evidence
  that the symbol's quantities are denominated in more than one share unit. On
  the committed IB2026 statement exactly one symbol qualifies: **LQQ, ratio
  218.10** (€9.069 … €1,977.94). The next-highest symbol in the statement
  (NFLX, 1.40) does not, and neither does any other.

- [x] AC2 — Prices in **different currencies are never compared** to form the
  ratio. The ratio is computed within each currency and the maximum across
  currencies is taken, so a symbol traded in both EUR and USD cannot be flagged
  by an FX difference it never experienced.

- [x] AC3 — **A split-inconsistent symbol's reconstructed quantity is withheld,
  not published.** It contributes **no** position line, **no** quantity and
  **no** market value on any day of the window — the engine never emits a
  199-unit LQQ position, and never values one. The phantom is the quantity, so
  withholding is of the quantity, not merely of its price.

- [x] AC4 — **The trade-price anchor refuses to carry a price across a detected
  discontinuity.** For a flagged symbol the anchor returns no price at all, at
  every date, independently of AC3 — so any future caller of the anchor is
  protected even if the quantity path changes. On IB2026 the €1,457.78 →
  $336,543 valuation of 2026-04-14 no longer exists.

- [x] AC5 — **The withholding is disclosed and named, not silent.** Run
  metadata carries a `quantity_withheld_symbols` entry per affected symbol with
  the symbol, the reason (`share_unit_discontinuity`), the currency, the low
  and high observed trade prices, the measured ratio, and the reconstructed
  opening quantity that was rejected. On IB2026 that is exactly one entry:
  `LQQ`, EUR, 9.069, 1977.94, 218.10, 199.0.

- [x] AC6 — A withheld symbol is **not** reported in any of the three valuation
  tiers. On IB2026, LQQ leaves both `unpriced_replay_symbols` and
  `trade_price_anchored_symbols` (it is currently in both — PRD F-3) and appears
  only in the withholding list. `unpriced_replay_symbols` becomes empty and
  `trade_price_anchored_symbols` becomes `['BTEC', 'ICHN', 'IUFS', 'IUHC',
  'ZPRV']`.

- [x] AC7 — **The withheld symbol's cash movements are preserved.** Its BUY and
  SELL cash effects still hit the replayed cash balance — those are broker
  truth and unaffected by the unit ambiguity — and, because the symbol is
  priced on no day, the existing US-24.9 trade-leg gate excludes its legs from
  `trade_flow`, so no return is fabricated from a trade that never crossed the
  market-value boundary.

- [x] AC11 — **A withheld symbol's cash never becomes a return.** Its trades
  still settle (AC7), but the position behind them is in no market value, so a
  cash-inclusive chain would publish the step as performance — the US-24.9
  fabrication class, re-opened by withholding. The day's flow is recorded as
  `DailyPortfolioState.unbacked_cash_flow` and a material amount makes the day's
  return **unpublishable on every basis**, disclosed with a reason naming this
  cause specifically. On IB2026 that withholds LQQ's six trade dates and removes
  a fabricated **+3.08%** on 2026-04-17 — the window's largest cash-inclusive
  TWR move — taking annualised TWR volatility from 15.49% to **14.18%**.

  > **Added 2026-08-13, mid-US-33.4.** This AC was not in the story as written.
  > The gap was caught by US-24.9's own de-dilution tripwire while re-measuring
  > for the adoption: the trade-neutral / cash-inclusive volatility ratio came
  > out at **×0.965** against a cash-weight prediction of ×1.042 — the wrong
  > side of 1. AC7 was correct that cash must be preserved and correct that
  > `trade_flow` neutralises the cash-EXCLUDED basis; it did not follow through
  > to the cash-INCLUSIVE one. The tripwire existing at all is why this was
  > caught before the goldens were regenerated rather than after.

- [x] AC8 — **The published series returns to a plausible band.** With LQQ
  withheld, the replay's peak `total_market_value` on the committed statement
  falls from **$518,078.75** (2026-07-06) to **$65,377.31** (2026-08-10),
  against a statement `stock_total` of **$64,922.99**, and the terminal state
  lands within **$12** of it — pinned, so the 8× inflation cannot return
  unnoticed. (Measured on the FX-enabled replay, the path the PRD's $518,078
  came from; the `fx_history={}` audit harness shows the same defect as
  $455,072.59 → $62,859.24.)

- [x] AC9 — The researcher **sees the withholding on screen**: the Dashboard's
  `ReplayDisclosuresCard` renders a note naming each withheld symbol, the
  evidence (observed price range and ratio), and the consequence — that the
  holding is absent from the replayed market value rather than valued. The card
  still renders **nothing** when no symbol is withheld and no other disclosure
  is present.

- [x] AC10 — **Behaviour is unchanged where there is no discontinuity.** A
  snapshot whose symbols all trade within the threshold produces byte-identical
  states, the same three-tier assignments and an empty
  `quantity_withheld_symbols` — the detection is a real signal, not a banner
  that always fires.

## Out of scope

- **Inferring the split ratio and adjusting quantities.** Reconstructing 199
  pre-split units into their post-split equivalent would be fabricating broker
  truth from a price jump. The honest answer is withholding (PRD non-goal).
- **A corporate-action data source or model** — splits, spin-offs, mergers,
  symbol changes. This story makes the replay safe in their presence, not
  correct through them.
- **US-33.3** — the tier-exclusivity contract wording. It is a separate story;
  note that AC6 here *reduces* the overlap on this statement but does not make
  the "exactly one tier" claim true in general.
- **US-33.4** — adopting the 2026-08-11 statement, re-deriving the ~30 stale
  value pins, and regenerating the goldens. Do not regenerate
  `dashboardGoldens.ts` or `golden_market_data.json` in this story.
- Detecting a discontinuity in the **snapshot/positions** path. Only the ledger
  replay reconstructs quantities, so only it can fabricate one.

## Notes / decisions

- **No academic precedent applies, and none should be cited.** This is a
  data-integrity guard, not a financial formula: it computes no return, risk or
  exposure number. Its precedent is internal — US-31.3 (fail closed on an
  untrustworthy cash anchor) and US-24.10 (disclose a weaker valuation tier
  rather than publish $0 silently). The methodology entry must present it as a
  **coverage/trust rule**, not as a model.

- **Threshold rationale (5.0).** Measured on the committed statement: the
  highest legitimate within-symbol, within-currency trade-price ratio is
  **1.40** (NFLX); LQQ is **218.10**. 5.0 sits ~3.6× above the highest
  legitimate observation and ~44× below the true positive. It is a heuristic
  policy value with no academic basis and is tunable only as a reviewed change
  (US-24.2 discipline).

- **Stated limitation — sub-threshold splits are not detected.** A 2:1 or 3:1
  split produces a ratio below 5.0 and will pass undetected, still summing
  mixed units. Lowering the threshold to catch it would start flagging
  genuinely volatile holdings over long windows, which would withhold real
  positions. The honest position is: this guard catches the catastrophic case
  (large-ratio unit changes) and is documented as not catching the small one;
  closing that gap properly needs a corporate-action source, which is an
  explicit epic non-goal. **This limitation must appear in the methodology
  section**, not only here.

- **Why withhold the quantity rather than the price.** Valuing 199 phantom
  units at $0 (the pre-US-24.10 behaviour) is also wrong — it publishes a
  position size the researcher never held. The fabricated object is the
  quantity, so the quantity is what is withheld.

- **Commit-gate reality (resolved).** When this story landed the suite was red
  with 32 backend failures, ~30 of them value pins stale against the refreshed
  statement. US-33.2 was therefore verified on its own test surface and
  committed together with US-33.3 and US-33.4, which re-derived those pins and
  regenerated the goldens against this fixed engine.

- **Evidence in this story was reproduced**, not inferred: the ratio table, the
  148-state window, the 130 contaminated LQQ days, the 455,072.59 → 62,859.24
  peak, and the tier lists were all measured against the committed statement
  and the frozen `golden_market_data.json` with `fx_history={}` (the audit
  harness's configuration). Test pins asserted through the dashboard-history
  path, which supplies real FX, will differ in the last digits — re-measure
  there rather than copying these numbers across paths.
