## Story statement

**As a** portfolio researcher with a Freedom24 statement,
**I want** the Dashboard to value my holdings from the dates the statement
actually gives, with each commission counted once,
**so that** the performance I read reflects the account I held, not holdings
that were never held on those dates.

## Context

Closes findings F1 and F7 (tech-debt register rows F1, F7; `02-quant-audit.md`
§ Findings F1, F7). The Freedom24 importer dates every trade and the period fee
to the first of the statement-start month. For FF2026 that puts 9 VTI in the
ledger from 2025-12-01, a date before the statement period, when the account
held 28 SCHD, 3 VTI and 4 VWO until 2026-04-07. The Dashboard replay then starts
on that fabricated date and the committed golden shows TWR -1.20%. Separately the
6.76 of commissions is charged twice: once inside each trade's net amount and
once as a period fee row, so the ledger cash roll-forward gives 45.28 against
the statement's 52.04.

Requirements carried from the research brief (`02-quant.md` § F1), which the
design lane must work within:

- A ledger date is the statement's own per-record date. A Transactions record is
  dated by its trade date, never its settlement date (trade-date accounting, as
  the IB CSV importer does). An unparseable date drops that record under the
  importer resilience rule; no date is ever synthesised from the statement
  period, its start, or a constant.
- Period totals with no per-record date ("Total commissions for the period") are
  not dated ledger entries. Commission is charged once.
- F1 alone gives TWR 3.58%, which is a known-wrong number because the double
  charge remains. The expected values below therefore need F1 and F7 shipped
  together, and the golden is regenerated once, after both.

D-4: the Dashboard replay window continues to start at the first ledger date. It
is not anchored at the statement-period start in this story. After the fix the
FF2026 window is 2026-03-31 to 2026-04-10, 8 valuation days, where the
committed golden shows 90. This is honest but will look like a shrink to a user
comparing before and after; the existing window-start disclosure is what tells
them why.

Truth classes: importer output is Broker Truth; the Dashboard figures are a
replay of current holdings against historical prices and stay Synthetic History
with trust `degraded` (basis `replay_derived`). This story does not change that
class or lift the trust level.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Trade date, not settlement.** For FF2026 the SCHD sell, VTI buy and VWO sell
   are each dated 2026-04-07, not their settlement date of 2026-04-08.
2. **No fabricated dates remain.** The set of dates in the FF2026 ledger is
   exactly 2026-03-31, 2026-04-01 and 2026-04-07; no entry is dated 2025-12-01 or
   any other date the statement does not state for it.
3. **Unparseable trade date drops the record.** A Transactions record whose trade
   date cannot be parsed is not imported and is not given any substitute date;
   the rest of the statement still imports.
4. **Commission is charged once.** The FF2026 ledger carries total commissions of
   6.76, not 13.52, and each commission is attributed to its trade's date
   2026-04-07 (2.56, 2.12 and 2.08 for the three orders).
5. **Cash closes to the statement.** The FF2026 ledger cash roll-forward gives
   52.04 (911.23 - 861.08 - 6.76 + 8.65), matching the statement's ending cash;
   it was 45.28.
6. **Replay window.** The FF2026 Dashboard replays 2026-03-31 to 2026-04-10, 8
   valuation days, and reports 2026-03-31 as the window start.
7. **Return.** For FF2026 on the frozen prices the Dashboard shows time-weighted
   return 3.81% and money-weighted return 3.81%.
8. **Range start and end values.** For every range tab the start value is
   2,955.01 and the end value is 3,071.00, with investment gain 112.48.
9. **Benchmark comparison.** The benchmark return is 4.48% and the excess return
   is -0.67 percentage points.
10. **Ranges are identical, and say why.** 1M, 3M, YTD, 1Y and All show identical
    values because the window is shorter than one month, and each discloses the
    2026-03-31 window start (D-4).
11. **Trust is not raised.** The FF2026 return trust is `degraded`.
12. **Reconciles fully.** The terminal reconciliation adjustment is +3.51 (9 VTI
    x (335.44 statement close - 335.05 frozen close)) and the cash-anchor
    residual is 0.00.
13. **Monthly grid stays withheld.** FF2026's monthly returns are still reported
    as unreliable and the grid is hidden, because only one month exists.
14. **Other statements unchanged.** Dashboard figures for IB2026 and ESPP2026 are
    identical before and after this story.

No AC asserts equality with the statement's own +5.89% NAV return. That figure
covers 2025-12-31 to 2026-04-11; with the D-4 anchor this replay covers 8 days,
so the two are different quantities. Pinning equality would fail on a residual
this story does not own (`01-product.md` § Stories, US-46.2 invest note).

## Test plan

Names the files and behaviours; individual test names and counts are the test
lane's to determine.

**Backend (pytest, `services/quant-engine/app/tests/`)**

- `test_importer.py`: the assertion that currently pins the fabricated
  `{"2025-12-01", ...}` date set (line ~288) is replaced with the
  2026-03-31 / 2026-04-01 / 2026-04-07 set.
- Freedom24 importer tests over the committed FF2026 statement: trade date used
  over settlement date; commissions total 6.76 counted once and dated to the
  trade; ledger cash roll-forward 52.04.
- A synthetic Freedom24 fixture with an unparseable trade-date cell: the record
  is dropped, no date is invented, the remaining records import. The dropped
  case is the resilience rule's regression test.
- A dashboard-history test for FF2026 on frozen prices pinning the AC 6-13
  values.

**Golden and frozen-market capture**

- The FF block of `apps/desktop/src/test/dashboardGoldens.ts` and
  `services/quant-engine/app/scripts/golden_market_data.json` are regenerated.
  The replay now requests 2026-03-31 to 2026-04-11, which the frozen capture does
  not hold, so the frozen market data raises by design until it is re-captured.
  **The re-capture needs `FMP_API_KEY` and network access**: a human-run step
  (`export_dashboard_goldens --capture`), not runnable by a lane in the frozen
  suite. FF series shrink from 90 to 9 rows per symbol and may trip the
  capture-regression guard; the `--allow-smaller-capture` override is expected
  to be needed and its use is a human decision (`02-quant.md` § F1, handoff).
- The regenerated golden is reviewed against AC 6-13 before it is accepted, not
  accepted because it is what the code produced.

**Frontend (vitest, `apps/desktop/`)**

- Golden-driven Dashboard tests for FF2026 pick up the regenerated values; the
  ranges-identical case and the window-start disclosure are asserted.
