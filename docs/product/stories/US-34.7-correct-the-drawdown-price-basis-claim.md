# US-34.7: Correct the drawdown price-basis claim, and disclose where it is real

**Epic:** Epic 34 — An Answerable Dashboard: Reachable Trust States
**PRD:** [`epic-34-answerable-dashboard-and-reachable-trust.md`](../prd/epic-34-answerable-dashboard-and-reachable-trust.md)
**Status:** Done (2026-08-13)
**Last updated:** 2026-08-13

## Story

As a **portfolio researcher**, I want the reason my drawdown is withheld to be
a true statement about my data, so that a documented justification is something
I can rely on rather than something that merely sounds careful.

## Context

US-34.2 pulled the drawdown out of its scope and left a note — in the story and
in a code comment on `_allow_dashboard_drawdown_outputs` — claiming that

> a drawdown chained from unadjusted closes is a *price* drawdown, which
> overstates the loss on dividend-paying holdings

**On the Dashboard's path that claim is false**, and it has been sitting in the
codebase as a justification ever since. Checking it is what this story is for.

### F-11 (Medium) — the Dashboard drawdown does not have the exposure its gate cites

`_compute_max_drawdown` chains `_portfolio_time_weighted_return_series` on the
**`portfolio_value`** basis, over the imported replay's daily states. On that
path dividends arrive as **ledger cash**, so the ex-date price drop is offset by
the receipt and the chain is already total-return-like.

Verified in the states themselves:

```text
2026-01-14   cash 4,676.82 -> 4,688.09   (+11.27; dividends that day 11.52 gross)
2026-01-16   cash 4,240.40 -> 4,244.11   (+3.71;  dividends that day  4.36 gross)
2026-02-13   cash 5,077.34 -> 5,081.01   (+3.67;  dividends that day  4.89 gross)
```

(the small shortfall each time is the withholding tax, itself a ledger entry)

Over the window: **$125.72 gross, −$17.93 withholding, $107.79 net**, all of it
in the replayed cash balance. The unadjusted-close concern does not apply here.

### F-12 (Low, but real) — the exposure belongs to the Risk tab, which already ships

The **synthetic** path is a different construction:
`_build_synthetic_snapshot_history_states_with_coverage` applies *current
holdings* to historical prices with a **flat cash balance** (today's ending
cash, constant across the window) and **no ledger**. A dividend there appears
only as the ex-date price drop, with no offsetting receipt — so the drawdown
genuinely is overstated, by roughly the yield across the lookback.

That is the Risk tab's Drawdown Analytics card, which **already publishes**.

Measured on this portfolio the exposure is small: of 18 current holdings, only
**PYPL** both is held and paid a dividend in the window ($3.64 gross ≈ 0.006% of
NAV). The mechanism is real and scales with how dividend-heavy the current
holdings are and with the lookback — the card offers 252d and 504d windows —
but on the committed statement it is negligible, and saying so precisely is
better than either ignoring it or overstating it.

### Why the Dashboard gate cannot simply be opened

`drawdown_family` is one of `investor_economics_partial_unlock.withheld_families`,
and 22 assertions plus two named policy tests pin `max_drawdown_pct is None`.
Publishing it therefore runs into **F-10** — the same blocked policy decision
that parked US-34.5. This story does **not** open the gate; it corrects the
justification attached to it and discloses the exposure where it is real.

Implementer must read:
- PRD F-10 (the blocked policy), and US-34.2's AC7 scope-narrowing note — the
  text this story corrects.
- `app/services/dashboard_history_engine.py` → `_allow_dashboard_drawdown_outputs`,
  `_compute_max_drawdown`.
- `app/services/diagnostics_engine.py` →
  `_build_synthetic_snapshot_history_states_with_coverage` (the flat-cash
  construction).
- `docs/finance/financial-methodology.md` §Wealth Index and Drawdown.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — **The false justification is removed from the code.** The comment on
  `_allow_dashboard_drawdown_outputs` no longer claims a dividend-adjustment
  exposure the replay path does not have. It states the actual reason the gate
  is closed: `drawdown_family` is withheld by the investor-economics policy, and
  reopening it is blocked on F-10.

- [x] AC2 — **US-34.2's story note is corrected in place**, with a dated
  amendment recording that the original reasoning was wrong and why — the story
  file is a record of what shipped, so it is amended, never silently rewritten.

- [x] AC3 — **The methodology states which construction has the exposure.**
  §Wealth Index and Drawdown distinguishes the two paths: the replay chain
  captures dividends as ledger cash and is total-return-like; the synthetic
  chain applies a flat cash balance and no ledger, so it is a price drawdown and
  overstates losses by roughly the yield across the lookback.

- [x] AC4 — **The claim is test-backed, not just asserted.** A test proves the
  replay path captures dividend cash — dividend ledger entries land in the
  daily states' cash — so a future change that stopped applying them would fail
  rather than silently restore the exposure the docs used to claim.

- [x] AC5 — **The synthetic path's exposure is pinned as a bound.** A test
  measures which current holdings paid dividends in the window (PYPL alone) and
  asserts the resulting overstatement is under a documented threshold on this
  statement — so a future dividend-heavy portfolio surfaces rather than hides.

- [x] AC6 — **Nothing is published that was not published before.**
  `max_drawdown_pct` stays `null` on every Dashboard range, the Risk tab is
  unchanged, and the goldens are byte-identical. This story changes claims and
  tests, not outputs.

## Out of scope

  decision that parked US-34.5. Publishing `max_drawdown_pct` would breach
  `withheld_families` and move 22 pinned assertions.
- **Fixing the synthetic path's dividend exposure.** That needs adjusted closes

  story bounds and discloses the exposure; it does not remove it.
- **Changing the Risk tab's drawdown output or its lookback windows.**

## Notes / decisions

- **No new formula.** Both drawdown constructions already exist and are
  unchanged. What changes is the documented claim about which one has a
  dividend exposure, plus tests that hold the claim to account.
- **Why a bound rather than a correction for F-12.** Correcting the synthetic
  drawdown needs total-return prices, which the provider does not currently
  supply (F-9). A measured bound is honest and available now; a correction is
  not.
- **This story exists because a justification went unchecked.** The claim in
  US-34.2 was written in good faith while narrowing that story's scope, and
  read plausibly for eight days. It was wrong about the path it was attached to.
  That is the same failure mode as Epic 33 F-3 and Epic 32's findings — an
  agent-facing statement asserting something the code does not do — which is
  why AC4 requires the corrected claim to be test-backed rather than merely
  rewritten.
