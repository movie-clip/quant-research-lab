## Story statement

**As a** portfolio researcher,
**I want** a position whose statement gives no cost basis to show its cost basis
and unrealized P&L as unavailable,
**so that** I never read an invented cost, or a zero P&L, as something my broker
reported.

## Context

Closes finding F4 (tech-debt register row F4; `02-quant-audit.md` § Findings F4),
extended by a second fabrication site the research brief found.

Two places invent the values today (`02-quant.md` § F4):

1. The Freedom24 importer emits VTI cost basis 3,018.96 (ending quantity times
   close) and unrealized P&L 0.00. FF2026 states neither. Six of its nine VTI were
   bought in-period and the cost of the three opening shares appears nowhere in
   the PDF, so no partial derivation is honest either.
2. The snapshot builder used on the request path sets cost basis equal to market
   value and unrealized P&L to zero for every position. It feeds the Exposure,
   stress, drawdown, distribution and drift engines, including Exposure for an
   added-statement node. On IB2026 that request path shows total cost basis
   62,843.22 and total unrealized P&L 0.00 against the statement path's 62,255.14
   and 3,491.53. This second site is not in the register.

Valuable-criterion caveat, stated plainly: the audit found no component that
renders the fabricated value today, so a user cannot perceive it yet. It is kept
as a story because guardrail 4 forbids the fabrication, D-1 named F4, and the
value is already persisted in saved imports. Its user-visible carrier is the
overview totals and top-positions numbers that consume it.

Requirements carried from the research brief (`02-quant.md` § F4, E4):

- Missing means unavailable: null, never derived from market value, close price,
  ending quantity times price, or partial trade history, and never zero-filled.
  The trust level is `unavailable`, not `withheld` (nothing is held back) and not
  `degraded` (there is no value to degrade).
- Aggregates are unavailable when any contributing position's value is unavailable,
  following the null-propagation precedent in § Multi-Statement Snapshot Merge. A
  partial-coverage aggregate would be a new labelled rung needing the owner's
  approval and is not built here.
- No Dashboard or Risk number consumes cost basis (grep-verified in the brief), so
  no such number should move.

Schema and contract: `ImportedPosition` cost basis and unrealized P&L become
optional, with matching changes in the desktop types and the overview fields; no
imports contract document exists yet. The exact shapes are the design lane's.

Truth classes: cost basis and unrealized P&L are Broker Truth when stated;
request-path snapshots carry none, so they are unavailable there. This story does
not mix the two.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Freedom24 position.** For FF2026, VTI's cost basis and unrealized P&L are
   unavailable, not 3,018.96 and 0.00.
2. **Trust level.** The unavailable cost basis and P&L carry trust `unavailable`,
   not `withheld` or `degraded`.
3. **FF overview totals.** For FF2026 the overview's total cost basis and total
   unrealized P&L are unavailable (they were 3,018.96 and 0.00).
4. **Top positions.** In FF2026's top-positions list, VTI's unrealized P&L is
   unavailable.
5. **Combined totals.** For IB2026 plus FF2026 the overview's total cost basis and
   total unrealized P&L are unavailable (they were 65,274.10 and 3,491.53, the
   latter silently IB-only).
6. **IB unchanged.** For IB2026 alone, total cost basis remains 62,255.14 and total
   unrealized P&L remains 3,491.53.
7. **Request path.** Exposure for an added-statement node, which is built without
   statement-stated cost basis, shows cost basis and unrealized P&L as
   unavailable, not market value and 0.00 (IB2026 was 62,843.22 and 0.00).
8. **Merge.** When two statements are combined, a position's cost basis and
   unrealized P&L are summed only if both statements state them; if either is
   unavailable, the combined value is unavailable.
9. **Rendering.** Anywhere the desktop shows these values, an unavailable one
   renders as unavailable (a dash or an explicit label), never as 0 or 0.00.
10. **Opening-lot cost.** When cost basis is unavailable, lot reconstruction for
    FF2026's three opening VTI carries no estimated unit cost (it was 335.44,
    derived from the fabricated basis).
11. **Saved workspaces are recomputed on load (D-5).** A workspace saved before
    this fix with VTI cost basis 3,018.96 and P&L 0.00 shows them as unavailable
    after it is opened, because the values are re-derived from the stored
    statement.
12. **No performance number moves.** Dashboard and Risk figures for IB2026 and
    FF2026 are identical before and after this story (measured from the state after
    US-46.2 for FF2026).

## Test plan

Names the files and behaviours; individual test names and counts are the test
lane's to determine.

**Backend (pytest, `services/quant-engine/app/tests/`)**

- Freedom24 importer test (`test_importer.py` area): FF2026 VTI has no cost basis
  or P&L; the schema accepts their absence.
- Overview tests: FF2026 totals and VTI top-position P&L unavailable; IB2026
  totals unchanged at 62,255.14 / 3,491.53; IB2026 + FF2026 totals unavailable.
- A request-path test on the snapshot builder: an added-statement IB2026 snapshot
  yields unavailable cost basis and P&L, and a grep-style guard that the
  market-value-as-cost computation is gone (both fabrication sites, per the
  brief's "grep for the computation, not the name").
- Merge test (`statement_importer` combine): sums only when both non-null.
- Ledger lot-reconstruction test: no opening-lot cost estimate when basis is
  unavailable.
- A regression test that Dashboard history and Risk outputs for IB2026 and FF2026
  are unchanged by the null.
- The `EnrichedPosition` nullable-or-remove outcome is decided by the design lane
  and tested accordingly (no production caller; dead-code gate applies).

**Frontend (vitest, `apps/desktop/src/`)**

- The overview types and any component rendering the totals or top-position P&L
  show unavailable, not 0, for null.
- A persistence test: a workspace fixture saved with the fabricated 3,018.96 / 0.00
  (build it from the pre-fix saved shape) shows unavailable after load (D-5).
- `docs/contracts/` field-parity coverage, if the repo has a parity test, for the
  newly nullable fields.
