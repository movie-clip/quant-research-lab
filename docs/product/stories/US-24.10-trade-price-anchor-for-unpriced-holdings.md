# US-24.10: Stop unpriced-symbol cash events fabricating investor-performance returns

**Epic:** Epic 24 — Codebase Improvement
**PRD:** [`epic-24-codebase-improvement.md`](../prd/epic-24-codebase-improvement.md)
**Status:** Done
**Last updated:** 2026-08-09

## Story

As a **portfolio researcher**, I want a holding the replay cannot price to be
valued at **the price my broker actually traded it at**, so that buying or
selling it moves market value instead of silently draining cash — and my
Dashboard stops reporting a −7.90% day on which nothing was bought or sold at a
loss.

## Context

Tech-debt register row **US-24.10**, logged by US-24.9 (2026-08-09) and rated
**High**. It is a live fabrication on the committed portfolio, in the truth
class the product presents as broker truth.

`unpriced_replay_symbols` (US-31.2 / Epic 31 F-1) records symbols the replay
holds on some day with **no** fetchable price history **and** no statement close
price to anchor on. They are disclosed — but they are still valued at **$0**.
That is survivable while merely held; it is not survivable when they are
**traded**, because the trade moves real cash:

```text
BUY  an unpriced symbol → cash leaves, no market value arrives → PV falls
SELL an unpriced symbol → cash arrives from a $0 position      → PV rises
```

`total_portfolio_value` steps by the full trade amount with no offsetting
position, and the cash-inclusive TWR — the **investor-performance** basis the
Dashboard uses — publishes that step as performance. Reproduced against the
frozen `golden_market_data.json` (deterministic, network-free) on IB2026:

| Date | Unpriced-symbol cash movement | TWR published |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-04-08 | −$5,092.82 (buying BTEC + IUFS + IUHC) | **−7.90%** |
| 2026-04-27 | +$5,341.92 (selling IUFS + IUHC) | **+9.61%** |

Nothing was earned or lost on either day. These two days are the largest moves
in the entire window and they contaminate the Dashboard performance chart, TWR,
monthly returns and max drawdown. Guardrail #3 (never publish a non-market move
as a return) — the same class as Epic 31 F-3, which withheld the terminal
reconciliation day.

**Why the fix is trade-price anchoring** (owner decision, 2026-08-09). The three
symbols are LSE-listed UCITS lines (`BTEC` IE00BYXG2H39, `IUFS` IE00B4JNQZ49,
`IUHC` IE00B43HR379) already carrying registry entries and resolution rules;
US-31.2 fetches them and the provider returns nothing. They are **round-trip
positions** — opened and fully closed inside the window — so they are absent
from `snapshot.positions` and therefore absent from `fallback_prices`, which is
built from the *current* holdings' `close_price`. But the statement records an
execution price for **every** trade (`ImportedLedgerEntry.price`, e.g. IUFS
15.115 on 2026-04-08). That is broker truth, already imported, needing no market
data. Carrying it forward makes the position worth roughly what was paid, so the
BUY and SELL legs cancel in `PV` and no fabricated return exists to withhold.

**The guardrail that shapes the design:** US-27.7 established that the replay
must **never back-fill a price before a symbol's first observation** — that
fabricates a market price for a date the market never produced one. The
trade-price anchor therefore carries **forward only**, from the first trade
onward. Verified sufficient for IB2026: all three symbols have opening quantity
**0.0** and a BUY as their first event, so forward-carry covers every day they
are held. A symbol *held at window start* with no history, no statement close
and no prior trade stays unpriced and disclosed — see Out of scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — A symbol with **no** price history and **no** statement close price
  is valued, on every day at or after its first broker trade, at the **most
  recent trade price at or before that day** taken from the statement's own
  ledger entries. It contributes that value to `total_market_value` instead
  of `0`.
- [x] AC2 — **No back-fill (US-27.7).** On days *before* a symbol's first
  observed trade it remains unvalued and disclosed — the anchor never
  reaches backwards to fabricate a price for a date the broker produced none.
- [x] AC3 — **Precedence is explicit and unchanged for existing cases:** real
  price history wins; then the statement close price (US-27.7 / US-30.2
  anchoring); then the last trade price. A symbol that is priced or
  statement-anchored today behaves **exactly** as before.
- [x] AC4 — **The fabricated returns are gone.** On IB2026 the 2026-04-08 and
  2026-04-27 investor-performance TWR returns are ordinary market-sized moves,
  not −7.90% / +9.61%; both are pinned explicitly, and the window's maximum
  absolute TWR daily return falls accordingly.
- [x] AC5 — **The degradation is disclosed, not hidden.** Trade-price-anchored
  symbols are surfaced on the run metadata as their own tier, distinct from
  `statement_anchored_symbols` and `unpriced_replay_symbols`, with a stated
  meaning: the segment is **flat between trades** and carries no market
  movement. A symbol appears in exactly one of the three tiers.
- [x] AC6 — **`unpriced_replay_symbols` shrinks honestly.** A symbol that the
  trade-price anchor now values is no longer reported as unpriced (it is
  reported in the new tier instead); a symbol the anchor still cannot value
  **stays** in `unpriced_replay_symbols`. The disclosure never empties by
  reclassification alone.
- [x] AC7 — **Currency is the trade's own.** The anchored value is converted
  from the currency the trade settled in (`ImportedLedgerEntry.currency`), not
  the fund currency the US-31.5 `valuation_currency` rule selects for
  market-priced holdings — a trade price is quoted in the currency it executed
  in. With no FX rate available it is carried unconverted and disclosed via the
  existing `fx_fallback_currencies` tier, never converted 1:1.
- [x] AC8 — **The risk-statistic family stays correct.** The US-24.9
  trade-neutral basis continues to exclude trades in genuinely unvalued
  symbols, and now *includes* the newly-valued ones (their legs are real market
  value again), with no second definition of "priced".
  *(Mechanism corrected during implementation: the AC assumed `is_valued` at
  trade time would remain the shared gate. It cannot be — giving these symbols
  a valuation exposed two cases it gets wrong, including a same-day round trip
  that lands in no day's market value at all. The gate is now computed from the
  built state: a leg counts only if the symbol is priced in today's market value
  or was priced in the previous day's. Still one rule, one code path — and it
  subsumes US-24.9's original case. See Notes → "What implementation changed".)*
- [x] AC9 — **Reproducible and measured.** The before/after IB2026 figures
  (the two fabricated days, max daily TWR move, annualised volatility on both
  the investor-performance and risk bases, and terminal market value vs the
  statement's `stock_total`) are recorded in the slice log. Terminal
  reconciliation must not worsen: the US-31.5 residual against `stock_total`
  ($1.35) stays within `REPLAY_RECONCILIATION_TOLERANCE`.
- [x] AC10 — `dashboardGoldens.ts` is regenerated **deliberately** and the diff
  itemised per family: market-value, portfolio-value, return, monthly-return
  and drawdown series may move; **statement-identity, weight, sector,
  cost-basis, ledger-count, ISIN and cash-by-currency families are
  byte-identical**. `golden_market_data.json` is byte-identical (no re-capture
  — this uses statement data already imported, no new symbols).

## Out of scope

- **Symbols held at window start with no history, no statement close and no
  prior trade.** Forward-carry cannot reach them without back-filling, which
  US-27.7 forbids. They remain `$0`-valued and disclosed via
  `unpriced_replay_symbols`, exactly as today. IB2026 has none (all three
  affected symbols open at quantity 0.0), so this is a latent case; if a future
  statement hits it, the honest fix is to **withhold** the affected days
  (the Epic 31 F-3 pattern), not to back-fill.
- **Fetching real prices** for these lines, or substituting the registered
  proxies (XLF / XLV / IBB). A proxy is a different security — the SEMI F-5
  wrong-instrument class (US-31.4). Proxies stay labeled and unused for
  valuation.
- **Modelling the bid/ask or commission component** of a trade price. The
  execution price is used as-is; the fee portion already flows through cash.
- **Changing the return basis of any surface.** US-24.9 settled that; this
  story only changes what a position is *worth*.
- **Interpolating between two trade prices.** The segment is deliberately flat
  and disclosed as such — interpolation would fabricate intermediate market
  movement.
- **Rendering the replay disclosures on the Dashboard.** None of the five are
  rendered today; surfacing them is its own story (**US-24.11**), not a
  side-effect of this one. See the T-24.10.3 note.

## Notes / decisions

- **Owner decision (2026-08-09):** of the four options put forward — withhold
  the affected days; value at last broker trade price; fetch real prices; or
  trade-price with a withhold fallback — the owner chose **value at last broker
  trade price**. Rationale: it fixes the root cause rather than papering over
  the symptom, needs no market data, is deterministic, and keeps the
  performance series gap-free. The withhold fallback is recorded in Out of
  scope for the latent case it would cover.
- **Why the flat segment is acceptable here but interpolation is not.** A
  carried trade price is a *price the broker actually observed*; every day it is
  carried is honestly labelled as carrying no market movement. An interpolated
  price is a number no one ever observed. This is the same line §Synthetic
  History Coverage Rule already draws for the statement-close anchor.
- **Precedence subtlety (do not invert it).** The statement close price
  outranks the trade price even though a trade may be *closer in time*, because
  changing that would alter the valuation of every currently-held
  statement-anchored symbol and regress US-27.7 / US-30.2 behaviour. The trade
  price is strictly a **new** tier for symbols that have neither of the other
  two — hence AC3's two-directional pin.
- **Why `asset_currency` is not the conversion basis.** On IB2026 every trade
  entry has `asset_currency=None` and `cash_currency="USD"`; the importer
  populates the settle currency, not the asset's. AC7 therefore specifies the
  trade's own settle currency, which is also the currency its `price` is
  quoted in.
- **This is a valuation-accuracy fix, and it is not small.** The two affected
  days are the largest moves in the IB2026 window. Expect the Dashboard's
  headline volatility and max drawdown to fall materially — that is the
  fabrication leaving, not the portfolio changing.
- **Academic precedent** (for the methodology section — this is a valuation
  convention, not a new formula):
  - CFA Institute (2020). *Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS) for
    Firms*, §2.A.1–2.A.2 — portfolios must be valued at fair value using
    objective, observable inputs, and returns must be adjusted for external
    cash flows; an internal transfer between cash and holdings is not
    performance.
  - IFRS 13 *Fair Value Measurement*, §§72–90 — the fair-value hierarchy:
    quoted prices in active markets (Level 1) before other observable inputs
    (Level 2, which is where a recent broker execution price for the identical
    instrument sits). The precedence ladder in AC3 mirrors this hierarchy.
  - Bacon, C.R. (2008). *Practical Portfolio Performance Measurement and
    Attribution*, 2nd ed., Ch. 2 (Wiley) — valuation-date discipline and the
    treatment of flows in time-weighted return.


### What implementation changed (recorded at close-out)

**A second fabrication class, found by this story's own new test and fixed
here.** US-24.9 gated `trade_flow` on "is this symbol valued today". Giving the
three symbols a valuation exposed two cases that predicate gets wrong, both
real on IB2026:

1. **Selling a symbol first observed by that very sale.** It is valued from the
   sale day onward, but it was never in *yesterday's* market value, so there is
   no leg to cancel.
2. **A same-day round trip in a new symbol.** 2026-06-11 `IITU` is bought and
   fully sold before any close — quantity nets to zero, so it is in **no** day's
   market value at all. Counting its $1,443.00 buy leg produced **−3.45%**
   against an expected **−0.36%**.

A per-direction predicate handled (1) but not (2). Both fall out of a single
rule computed from the built state rather than guessed at beforehand: **a trade
leg may be neutralised only if the symbol is priced in today's market value or
was priced in the previous day's.** That replaced `is_valued`-at-trade-time as
the `trade_flow` gate, and it subsumes US-24.9's original unpriced-symbol case
as well — three special cases collapsed into one rule.

**Measured result on IB2026** (frozen data, network-free). The AC9 tripwire is
clean: with the fabricated days gone, the trade-neutral chain now tracks a
cash-de-diluted TWR to within **0.0537% on every one of the 117 days** — no
exclusions needed, where US-24.9 had to exclude two.
