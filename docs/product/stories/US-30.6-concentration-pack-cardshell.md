# US-30.6: Migrate the Concentration Pack onto CardShell + bring it under the design-system audit

**Epic:** Epic 30 — Exposure Improvements
**PRD:** [`epic-30-exposure-improvements.md`](../prd/epic-30-exposure-improvements.md)
**Status:** Done
**Last updated:** 2026-07-16

## Story

As a **portfolio researcher using a screen reader (or any keyboard user)**, I
want the Concentration Pack — the largest block on the Exposure tab — to be an
announced, navigable region like every other card, so that the biggest surface
on the tab is not an anonymous `<section>` my assistive tech skips over.

## Context

US-30.6 was scoped in the PRD as a generic "Exposure UI polish pass (design-system
audit against ui-polish baseline; a11y; empty/error states)". A findings-first
audit of the tab against the `ui-polish` DoD checklist (Epic 27/30 discipline)
found the tab **already meets the baseline** — the polish landed incrementally:

- All five design-system audit tests
  (`apps/desktop/src/test/designSystem.audit.test.ts`) pass.
- Every design-system card wraps in `CardShell`; every self-fetching card
  (Drift, Attribution, Correlation table, Heatmap) has `LoadingState` +
  `ErrorState` (the props-only cards correctly do not need them); every Recharts
  chart uses `ChartShell` + `chartDefaults`; both correlation surfaces carry the
  ▲▲/▲/•/▼/▼▼ glyph so colour is never the sole encoder.

The audit surfaced **one real gap**, exactly the migration `ui-polish` explicitly
defers ("the Concentration Pack is intentionally not migrated yet … a separate
future story"): the **Concentration Pack**, inline in `ExposurePanel.tsx`
(`:237-300`) — the largest Exposure surface by area — is:

1. **Three raw `<section>` elements with no landmark.** The outer block
   (`:237`) and the two inner "Top Positions" / "Top Sectors" subsections
   (`:259`, `:277`) have no `role="region"` / `aria-labelledby`, so a screen
   reader announces no navigable region for the biggest block on the tab. Every
   `CardShell`-wrapped card gets that landmark for free.
2. **Outside the audited surface.** `ExposurePanel.tsx` is **not** in
   `ALL_CARD_FILES`, so nothing prevents a future hex/px/`"Synthetic"`-literal
   regression in it (it is clean today — verified zero inline hex/px literals —
   but unprotected).

**Deliberately NOT changed (truth-class integrity — guardrail #2):** the
Concentration Pack is **snapshot analytics** (current-state composition from
broker-truth positions), **not synthetic history**. It correctly renders **no**
`Synthetic` badge, and this story must not add one — a `TrustBadge type="synthetic"`
here would be a truth-class error. Its `Basis` / `Scope` / `Availability` status
strip stays as-is (metadata chips over a snapshot-analytics availability signal
whose three states — available / partial / withheld — do not map to TrustBadge's
two types). This is a **landmark + regression-coverage** migration, **not** a
visual redesign and **not** a trust-badge change.

This is the **last story in Epic 30**; it closes the epic.

Implementer must read: `apps/desktop/src/features/portfolio/ExposurePanel.tsx`
(the Concentration Pack `:237-300`, the `SummaryMetric` / `UnavailablePanel`
helpers, `buildConcentrationAvailabilityState`); `app/primitives/CardShell.tsx`
(the landmark it provides + `className` passthrough); `.claude/skills/ui-polish/SKILL.md`
(Migration notes — the deferred-pack note, and "don't half-migrate"); the audit
`apps/desktop/src/test/designSystem.audit.test.ts` (`ALL_CARD_FILES` vs
`CARDS_WITH_BADGE` vs `CHART_FILES` — three distinct lists);
`ExposurePanel.test.tsx`.

## Notes / decisions

- **Why not "polish everything":** the findings-first audit showed the tab
  already meets the ui-polish baseline, so a broad pass would be churn. The one
  real, deferred gap is the Concentration Pack landmark + its absence from the
  audited surface — that is the whole story, matching `ui-polish`'s explicit
  "don't half-migrate" guidance by doing the *complete* focused migration of
  one surface rather than a partial sweep of many.
- **Why no TrustBadge:** honest truth-class labelling. The pack is broker-truth
  composition (snapshot analytics); a "Synthetic" badge would misstate its
  provenance. Its availability signal is a separate concern, already surfaced by
  the status strip, and deliberately left unchanged.
