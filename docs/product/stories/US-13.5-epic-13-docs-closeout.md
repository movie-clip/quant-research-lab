## Story

As a **portfolio researcher** (and as an agent picking up this product
six months from now), I want every Risk-tab field to be traceable from
the UI surface back to a backend schema, a methodology formula, and an
academic citation — and I want the canonical shipped-state inventory,
project CLAUDE.md, and epic roadmap to reflect the three-tab reality —
so that the Risk tab joins the Exposure tab as a fully-documented,
auditable surface and Epic 13 can close cleanly.

## Context

The Risk tab is feature-complete after US-13.1 → US-13.4 (all Done):
StressScenariosCard, DrawdownAnalyticsCard, VarDistributionCard sit in
`RiskPanel.tsx` with the design-system primitives, a11y baseline, and
density polish. What is missing is the canonical doc reconciliation:
no `risk-fields.md` contract doc exists yet, the methodology sections
still say "added in Epic 13" / "placeholder path", and `CLAUDE.md` +
`current-product-state.md` still describe a two-tab product.

This is a pure docs-reconciliation story. No code, no tests. After it
ships, Epic 13 closes.
