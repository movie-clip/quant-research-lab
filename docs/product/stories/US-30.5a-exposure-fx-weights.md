# US-30.5a: FX-convert every Exposure weight + disclose the currency basis

**Epic:** Epic 30 — Exposure Improvements
**PRD:** [`epic-30-exposure-improvements.md`](../prd/epic-30-exposure-improvements.md)
**Status:** Next phase
**Last updated:** 2026-07-08

## Story

As a **portfolio researcher**, I want every Exposure weight computed in my base
currency and the conversion basis stated on the card, so that my EUR and GBP
holdings are not silently underweighted and I can see exactly which rates
produced the numbers I am about to make decisions on.

## Context

PRD findings **F-7 (Critical)** and **F-8 (High)**, verified by US-30.4.

Every Exposure weight denominator raw-sums `position.market_value` across
EUR/GBP/USD with **no FX conversion anywhere** in the exposure path.
Reproduced on `docs/IB2026.csv`: raw **$58,588.76** vs FX-converted
**$61,238.53** — and the converted figure reproduces the statement's *own*
stock total to the cent, so the statement is the arbiter, not a modelling
preference (4.33% understatement). Weight errors: SEMI (GBP) **+1.24pp**,
SXRV (EUR) **+1.20pp**, VDST **−0.87pp**, VUAA **−0.85pp**; position **HHI
off −2.29%**. F-8: none of those cards discloses the degradation — a
confident `Synthetic` badge over currency-mixed numbers (the F-2 lesson).

The rates already exist as broker truth (`statement_totals.fx_rates`,
US-28.1) and are already plumbed to the drift engine (US-30.2). Two supply
paths must be closed:

- **Imported path** (`run_imported_diagnostics_engine`, golden pipeline):
  the real `ImportedPortfolioSnapshot` already carries
  `statement_totals.fx_rates`. Nothing to plumb.
- **Request path** (`ExposureEngineRequest` / `DiagnosticsEngineRequest`):
  `build_imported_snapshot_from_request` sets `statement_totals=None`, so the
  rates are lost. `fx_rates` moves up from `DriftEngineRequest` to the shared
  `PortfolioEngineRequest` and the builder materialises
  `ImportedStatementTotals(fx_rates=…)` **only when rates are supplied** —
  keeping `statement_totals=None` (and therefore byte-identical behaviour)
  when they are not. *Verified safe:* the only two `statement_totals`
  consumers on this path guard on specific fields
  (`portfolio_state.py:135` on `starting_nav`, `:259-266` on
  `ending_nav`/`cash_total`), both `None` here, so US-30.1's cash anchor and
  the terminal reconciliation are untouched.

Conversion policy follows the established US-27.8 / US-30.2 precedent: a
position whose currency has a rate is converted (disclosed as
`fx_static_rate_currencies` — a *static* period-end rate, never a claim of FX
dynamics); a position with no rate is **carried unconverted** (the only
honest number held) and disclosed as `fx_fallback_currencies`. A position is
never dropped from the denominator — that would hide it entirely.

**This story regenerates `dashboardGoldens.ts`** — deliberately. Every
IB2026-derived weight/HHI/sector/look-through value shifts. That is the
finding being fixed, not churn; the diff is itemized per-family in the slice
log (the Epic 28 convention).

Implementer must read: PRD F-7/F-8; `analytics/risk.py` (`:583`, `:631`,
`:652`, `:1049`, `:1987`), `analytics/overview.py:13`,
`services/exposure_engine.py:48,192`, `services/intra_correlation_engine.py:94`;
methodology §Risk Contribution and Concentration, §FX Conversion Fallback
Disclosure, §Currency Exposure; `docs/contracts/exposure-fields.md`.

## Notes / decisions

- **The statement is the arbiter.** The FX-converted total reproduces the
  statement's own `stock_total` to the cent, which is why F-7 is a bug and not
  a modelling preference. AC1/test (4) encode exactly that check, so a future
  refactor cannot quietly regress it.
- **Carry-unconverted over drop.** Dropping an unconvertible position from the
  denominator would silently shrink the portfolio and inflate every other
  weight. Carrying it raw is wrong-but-visible; the disclosure (AC3/AC5) is
  what makes it honest — the same choice US-27.8 made for the replay path.
- **No new formula**, so no citation required: this is a unit-correctness fix
  (convert before summing) applied to formulas already documented in
  §Risk Contribution and Concentration.
- Goldens: the regeneration is deterministic (frozen market-data fixture), so
  the diff is reviewable and reproducible on any machine.
