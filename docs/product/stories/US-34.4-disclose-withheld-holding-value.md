# US-34.4: Say what a withheld holding was worth, and stop withholding immaterial days

**Epic:** Epic 34 — An Answerable Dashboard: Reachable Trust States
**PRD:** [`epic-34-answerable-dashboard-and-reachable-trust.md`](../prd/epic-34-answerable-dashboard-and-reachable-trust.md)
**Status:** Done (2026-08-13)
**Last updated:** 2026-08-13

## Story

As a **portfolio researcher**, I want to know how much of my portfolio the
replay is not showing me, and to keep the days where the gap is too small to
matter, so that a withholding is a measured fact I can size rather than a hole
of unknown depth.

## Context

PRD findings **F-3** and **F-4**. Both are consequences of Epic 33's
withholding being *correct but unquantified*.

**F-3 — the researcher is told a holding vanished, not how big it was.**
US-33.2 withholds LQQ's reconstructed quantity because a ~200:1 split makes it
untrustworthy. That is right. But the disclosure names the symbol and the
evidence for withholding, and says nothing about magnitude, so the researcher
cannot tell whether they are missing 0.1% of their book or 30% of it.

**The broker's own cash movements answer it without a price or a quantity** —
which matters, because the quantity is exactly what is untrusted. Tracking the
net cash the broker put into the symbol gives a lower bound on what was at
stake, from broker truth alone:

```text
2026-04-14  BUY   cash -1,684.16    net invested   1,684.16
2026-04-17  BUY   cash -1,809.54    net invested   3,493.70
2026-04-17  SELL  cash +3,607.98    net invested    -114.28   <- flat
2026-06-10  BUY / SELL  same day    net invested    -139.38   <- flat
2026-06-12  BUY   cash -2,264.88    net invested   2,125.50
2026-06-23  BUY / SELL  same day    net invested   2,130.62   <- peak, end of day
2026-07-17  SELL  cash +2,092.25    net invested      38.37   <- round trip closed
```

Peak end-of-day net investment **$2,130.62**, about **3.52%** of the portfolio
on the day it peaked, across a **66-day** exposure span (of 148). (The within-day gross peak reaches
$4,410.08 on 2026-06-23 because a buy precedes a sell on the same date; the
end-of-day figure is the honest daily bound.) The near-zero closing figure of
$38.37 independently confirms the position was fully exited — a useful sanity
signal that the cash tracking is sound.

**F-4 — the unbacked-cash guard withholds days that distort nothing.**
US-33.2 withholds any day whose unbacked cash flow exceeds
`REPLAY_RECONCILIATION_TOLERANCE` ($1.00), a constant calibrated for cent-level
rounding across daily states, not for materiality against a portfolio. The
result on IB2026 is bimodal, and two of the six withheld days are noise:

| Date | Unbacked flow | Portfolio | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-06-23 | $5.13 | $60,608 | **0.0085%** |
| 2026-06-10 | $25.09 | $62,710 | **0.0400%** |
| 2026-04-14 | −$1,684.16 | $60,893 | 2.7658% |
| 2026-07-17 | $2,092.25 | $62,515 | 3.3468% |
| 2026-04-17 | $1,798.44 | $63,433 | 2.8352% |
| 2026-06-12 | −$2,264.88 | $61,047 | 3.7101% |

The gap between the two groups is **69×**. US-33.4 recorded the flat tolerance
as a deliberate, reviewable choice; this is the review.

- `docs/finance/financial-methodology.md` §Share-Unit Discontinuity Withholding
  (the rule and its stated limitation).
- `app/engine/portfolio_state.py` → `detect_share_unit_discontinuities`,

- `docs/contracts/dashboard-fields.md` → `quantity_withheld_symbols` and

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — **A withholding says what it cost.** Each
  `quantity_withheld_symbols` entry carries the peak end-of-day net cash the
  broker had invested in that symbol, in base currency, and the span of
  valuation days over which the symbol was excluded. On IB2026: **$2,130.62**
  across **66** of the window's 148 days.

  > **Corrected during implementation.** The AC first said "the number of days
  > it carried a positive net investment", and the field was named
  > `held_day_count`. Both were wrong. Answering "days held" needs a running
  > QUANTITY — the untrusted thing — and cumulative cash cannot stand in for it:
  > a round trip that closes at a loss leaves a positive cash residual, so every
  > day after the position closed kept counting (44 by that measure against a
  > ~26-day true holding). The field is now `exposure_day_count`: the span from
  > the symbol's first trade to its last, which is unambiguous, derivable, and
  > answers what the researcher is actually asking — for how long was this
  > hidden from me.

- [x] AC2 — **The bound is derived from broker cash only.** No price, no
  quantity and no market data enter it — the quantity is the untrusted thing,
  so using it would defeat the purpose. Each trade's cash effect is
  FX-converted before it enters the running total (the US-31.3 trap).

- [x] AC3 — **The magnitude is expressed against the portfolio**, so the
  researcher can size it without arithmetic: the entry carries the peak net
  investment as a share of the portfolio on the day it peaked — **3.52%** on
  IB2026 — computed by the engine, never by the component (guardrail #5).

- [x] AC4 — **The researcher sees it.** `ReplayDisclosuresCard`'s withholding
  note states the amount and the share alongside the existing evidence, so the
  sentence answers "how much am I not seeing?" rather than only "why".

- [x] AC5 — **Immaterial unbacked-cash days keep their returns.** The guard
  fires on a share of portfolio value rather than a flat dollar amount. On
  IB2026 the 2026-06-10 ($25.09) and 2026-06-23 ($5.13) days become publishable
  again; 2026-04-14, 2026-04-17, 2026-06-12 and 2026-07-17 stay withheld.
  `withheld_return_dates` goes from 7 entries to **5**.

- [x] AC6 — **The threshold is documented with its measured margin**, lives in
  `app/core/constants.py`, and is stated as a heuristic policy value with no
  academic basis (US-24.2 discipline). The margin is 69× between the largest
  immaterial day (0.0400%) and the smallest material one (2.7658%).

- [x] AC7 — **The withheld-day disclosure stays honest.** The two recovered
  days are removed from `withheld_return_dates` because they are genuinely
  published, not because the disclosure was narrowed —
  `withheld_return_impact_pct` is re-measured over the days that remain.

- [x] AC8 — **Nothing else moves.** No valuation, no quantity, no cash balance
  and no market value changes: this story adds a measurement and relaxes one
  threshold. `total_market_value`, `total_portfolio_value` and every position
  field stay byte-identical in the goldens.

- [x] AC9 — **A run with no withholding is unaffected.** A snapshot with no
  discontinuity reports an empty `quantity_withheld_symbols` and no unbacked
  days, exactly as today.

## Out of scope

- **Inferring the split ratio or reconstructing the true quantity.** Epic 33's
  standing non-goal. This story bounds the position's *value* from cash; it does
  not resurrect the position.
- **Publishing the withheld symbol's market value.** The bound is a disclosure
  of magnitude, not a valuation, and must never enter `total_market_value`.
- **The reconciliation-adjustment withholding** (US-31.3) — its tolerance is a
  separate question, and the terminal day is F-8 / US-34.8.
- **Lowering the share-unit detection threshold** (Epic 33's documented
  limitation that a 2:1 split goes undetected).

## Notes / decisions

- **No new formula and no academic citation.** The bound is a running sum of
  broker cash effects; the materiality rule is a ratio. The methodology entry
  documents both as disclosure/trust rules, alongside the existing withholding
  rule they extend.
- **Why end-of-day, not the within-day gross.** On 2026-06-23 a buy precedes a
  sell, so the within-day gross peak ($4,410.08) overstates what was ever held
  overnight. The daily state is an end-of-day object, so the end-of-day figure
  ($2,130.62) is the one that matches what the replay reports.
- **Why a lower bound rather than an estimate.** Net cash invested is what the
  broker actually paid, not what the position was worth on any given day. It is
  therefore a *floor* on the magnitude, which is the honest direction to err:
  it can understate what the researcher is missing, never overstate it. The
  disclosure must be worded as "at least", not "worth".
- **Threshold margin (AC6).** Largest immaterial day 0.0400%; smallest material
  day 2.7658%; 69× apart. 0.1% sits ~2.5× above the noise and ~28× below the
  signal.
